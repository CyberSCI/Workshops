# Before the CTF

## 
## 1. Understand the rules 
## 2. Learn the infrastructure
## 3. Practice
## 4. Plan

---

## Understand the scoring rules

- Every competition scores differently
- How is uptime scored? 
- What happens if your service is down? Do you lose points? (Hint: Usually yes)
- How long are ticks?
- How long do you have before attacks start?

---

## Scout the infrastructure

- Containers or VMs? How does code get deployed?
- What are you allowed to install?
- For CyberSci, know Docker and Docker Compose basics

---

## Practice before the day

- This workshop gives you a starting point
- For more check out https://github.com/CyberSCI/PastChallenges
- We release our challenges after every competition

---

## Plan with your team

- Agree where you communicate during the event
- Are you going to split services between members? Or tackle services together?
- Who is going to actually apply the patches and deploy them?
