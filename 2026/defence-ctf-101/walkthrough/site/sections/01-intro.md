# What is a Defence CTF?

Any CTF where you are given a vulnerable service and you get points by identifying the vulnerability and patching it (rather than exploiting it)

---

## Types of Defence CTFs

### Jeopardy Competition - Defence Challenge:
- Automated attacks run against your service on demand
- Patch at any time -> get full points 

--- 
 
### Defence Competition

- Automated attacks run against your service every tick (e.g. every 2 minutes)
- You gain or lose points every tick 

--- 
### Attack & Defence Competition

- Automated health checks (SLAs) run every tick
- Other teams attack your services (lose points)
- You attack other team services (gain points)

---

## Where you'll see these formats

### Jeopardy Competition - Defence Challenge: **CyberSci Regionals**

--- 

### Defence Competition: **CyberSci Nationals**

--- 
### Attack & Defence Competition: **Internationally (ICC, ECSC)**
